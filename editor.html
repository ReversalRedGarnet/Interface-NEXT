Shapes

Arm a tool, click the canvas to place it. Escape returns to Select. Reposition anything by dragging it, or click it once and click a destination point instead. Drag a wall's or outline's small handles to reshape it; a rectangular shape's corner handles resize it (always staying a rectangle); an entrance is a fixed width and only moves, not resizes.
